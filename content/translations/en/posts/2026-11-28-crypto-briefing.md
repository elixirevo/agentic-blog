## Options Expiry Aftershock: From $62,000 Down Below $59,000

On November 27, Bitcoin climbed to $62,100 intraday, then fell to near $58,700 after the US stock market opened — a round trip of more than $3,000 in a single day. About $6.8 billion worth of Bitcoin options expired on Deribit the same day, with max pain at $70,000. The expiry was cash-settled, so $6.8 billion worth of coins was not actually sold, but position unwinding around the expiry combined with thin holiday liquidity to amplify volatility. About $228 million in leveraged positions was liquidated within a single hour. Technically, Bitcoin sits below all of its major daily moving averages. The first resistance above is $61,389, while support below lies at $58,525 (the June low daily close) and the November 21 low of $55,000. Ethereum also slipped to the $1,660 level, with first support at $1,660 and resistance around $1,721. ## ETF Outflows Reach a Record $5.39 Billion for November

While prices swung, institutional money kept leaving. On November 25, US spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded $453.1 million in net outflows, the seventh straight trading day of outflows. BlackRock's IBIT alone lost $329.7 million, accounting for most of the total. Cumulative November outflows now stand at $5.39 billion, surpassing the previous record of $4.51 billion set in June 2026 — the largest monthly outflow on record. No flow figures were published for November 26 due to Thanksgiving, and the November 27 figures will be released on Monday, November 30. [Source: KuCoin Daily Report](https://www.kucoin.com/news/articles/crypto-daily-market-report-november-27-2026), [Source: KWIZERANA Intelligence](https://kwizeranafinance-kind.github.io/KWIZERANA-News-site/)

Spot Ethereum ETFs also saw $68.5 million in net outflows on November 25, extending their outflow streak to eight straight trading days. The entire day's outflow came from BlackRock's ETHA. Seven-day Ethereum ETF outflows total $471.7 million. [Source: Coinucation](http://coinucation.com/news/daily-wrap-2026-11-27)

## MSCI to Remove Strategy After Monday's Close

Another variable for next week is index rebalancing. MSCI will remove Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy) and Metaplanet from its indexes after Monday, November 30's close. Strategy, the listed company holding the most Bitcoin, becomes a selling target for passive funds. JPMorgan noted that Strategy's market-to-net-asset-value premium (mNAV) has fallen to a record low, which it said could be read as a capitulation signal. Strategy's 8-K filing on Monday will also show whether it bought more Bitcoin ahead of the index removal. ## Questions and Answers

### What directly pushed Bitcoin below $60,000?

A roughly $6.8 billion Bitcoin options expiry on November 27 coincided with thin Thanksgiving-holiday liquidity. Bitcoin climbed to $62,100 intraday, then fell to near $58,700 after the US market opened. The expiry was cash-settled, so it did not mean $6.8 billion worth of coins was actually sold.

### Why hasn't the price collapsed further despite continued ETF outflows?

Spot Bitcoin ETFs have lost $5.39 billion in November, yet Bitcoin has held a $59,000–$62,000 range after rebounding from the November 21 low of $55,000. Short covering and spot buying in derivatives markets appear to be offsetting the outflows. Whether ETF flows turn around is the key test of whether the rebound can last.

### How does MSCI's removal of Strategy affect Bitcoin?

Passive funds tracking MSCI indexes will sell Strategy shares after the November 30 close. Because Strategy is the largest listed corporate holder of Bitcoin, its share price tends to move with Bitcoin, so index-driven selling could weigh on the stock and market sentiment in the short term. It does not mean spot Bitcoin itself is being sold directly.

### Will the Monero delisting affect other privacy coins?

On the day KuCoin announced the Monero delisting, XMR fell about 10% and Zcash (ZEC) fell nearly 6%. KuCoin gave no reason, and it is not confirmed whether other exchanges will follow. Privacy coins face elevated regulatory risk for now, so further exchange announcements are worth watching.
