## FOMC Minutes Confirm Hawkish Stance; December Hike Now a Near Certainty

The minutes of the FOMC's October 27–28 meeting, released early this morning, confirmed the market's fears about tightening. According to the minutes, "almost all participants" judged that "further action to raise the target range for the federal funds rate" would likely be appropriate "in the months ahead" to return inflation to the 2% target in a timely manner. At the October meeting, holding the benchmark rate at 3.75–4.00% passed by a 10–2 vote, with Governor Lisa Cook and Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack dissenting in favor of an immediate 25bp hike. ([TT News](https://www.ttnews.com/articles/fed-officials-back-rate-hike))

Some participants said an increase of more than 25 basis points could be considered at the December meeting. Fed Governor Christopher Waller publicly raised the possibility of a 50bp hike in December on November 18. According to the CME FedWatch tool, the market is pricing a rate hike at the December 8–9 meeting with 100% probability — about 95% for a 25bp move and about 5% for a 50bp move. ([The Apex Wire](https://theapexwire.com/finance/fed-minutes-december-rate-hike-signal/))

Inflation pressure also remains elevated. September PCE inflation rose 3.6% year over year, with core PCE up 3.1%. Notably, the minutes flagged the AI infrastructure buildout as a source of inflation risk. ([SourceFirst News](https://sourcefirstnews.com/fed-minutes-october-2026-rate-hikes-december-ai-inflation))

## ETF Outflows Resume; Institutional Exit Drives the Decline

Fund flows have worsened again. U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded $239.94 million in net outflows on November 17. BlackRock's IBIT alone lost $209.54 million, followed by Grayscale's GBTC (-$17.74 million) and Ark 21Shares' ARKB (-$13.12 million). The outflow came just one day after a $7.99 million net inflow on November 16 had ended a seven-session outflow streak, showing that institutional demand is still far from recovering. ([CoinGabbar](https://www.coingabbar.com/en/crypto-etf-news-today-btc-eth-xrp-outflows-fomc-minutes-meeting))

Ethereum ETFs also extended their losing run to three straight days with $29.09 million in net outflows, while XRP ETFs lost $6.14 million. Outflows from spot Bitcoin ETFs in November now exceed $2.58 billion. ([FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/cryptocurrencies/news/bitcoin-price-forecast-btc-stalls-as-etf-outflows-mount-ahead-of-fed-minutes-202611180758))
