## Nvidia CEO Resignation Spreads the Shock Across Risk Assets

Nvidia was the direct trigger for the sell-off. After Jensen Huang resigned on November 12, Nvidia shares plunged as much as 14% intraday and closed down 4.34% at $166.19. According to [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/12/nvidia-shares-plunge-10-as-jensen-huang-resigns), the stock fell as low as $148.80 during the session and its market capitalization dropped below $4 trillion. CFO Colette Kress was named interim CEO, and the board appointed Harvey Jones as its first-ever board chair.

The resignation followed a scandal surrounding Huang. According to [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/12/ai-titan-jensen-huang-out-as-nvidia-ceo), the board discussed Huang's future on the night of November 11 after investigations into his relationship with a person suspected of being a Chinese spy and another relationship inside the company, and the resignation was announced the next day. With Nvidia due to report earnings on November 17, uncertainty over succession and the continuity of its AI chip business is likely to linger.

The shock spilled into crypto as well. Shares of Bitcoin miners that have expanded into AI data centers also fell. A day when risk-off sentiment from Nvidia hit stocks and crypto alike.

## ETF Outflows for a Fifth Day; MSCI Drops Strategy

Flows are not helping either. U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded $299.8 million in net outflows on November 11, a fifth consecutive day of outflows. Cumulative outflows since November 5 total about $1.44 billion. Spot Ethereum ETFs lost $144.8 million on the same day.

MSCI also announced it will remove Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy) from the MSCI USA Index effective at the close on November 30. With Bitcoin trading below Strategy's average purchase cost of $75,456, concerns about passive outflows from the index removal added to the pressure. At the same time, JPMorgan raised its stake in Strategy by 36% and holds $540 million in Bitcoin ETFs — a mixed set of signals.

## Macro: Cooler CPI Failed to Spark a Rally

The next test comes tonight. October PPI is released at 8:30 a.m. ET on November 13 (10:30 p.m. KST), with consensus at +0.4% month over month and +0.3% for core. A hotter-than-expected print could further fuel hike concerns.

## Questions and Answers

### What happens if Bitcoin falls below $70,000?

$70,000 is a psychological support level near the June low zone. A break below it could open the way toward the $65,000–$69,000 range in the short term, according to technical analysis. That is an observation, not a guarantee of price direction.

### Why does the PPI release matter?

PPI is an inflation gauge that feeds into the Fed's rate decisions. A hotter-than-expected print could raise December hike odds and pressure risk assets, while a cooler print could ease hike concerns.

### Why are ETF outflows continuing?

Rate-hike concerns combined with falling prices are driving short-term money out of the funds. Five straight days of outflows total about $1.44 billion since early November, and that supply overhang is holding back any price recovery.

### What does the Nvidia situation have to do with crypto?

There is no direct link, but Nvidia is the symbol of the AI rally and is connected to mining firms that expanded into AI data centers. A sharp drop in a major growth stock weakens sentiment across risk assets, and that selling pressure spilled into volatile assets like Bitcoin.
