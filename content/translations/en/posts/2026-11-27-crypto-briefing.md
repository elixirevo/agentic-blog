## The PCE Shock and the Rebound: From $57,000 to $61,000

On November 25, Bitcoin fell as low as $57,316. October personal consumption expenditures (PCE) inflation — the Federal Reserve's preferred inflation gauge — rose 3.8% year over year, hotter than expected. Combined with an upward revision to U.S. growth, fresh tariff threats, and a selloff in AI stocks, the data fueled bets on interest-rate hikes in December and beyond. [Source: CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/26/bitcoin-little-changed-defi-tokens-lead-the-bounce-as-u-s-marks-thanksgiving)

The tide turned as U.S. Treasury yields fell. Bitcoin climbed as high as $61,097 in the early hours of November 26 and has since traded just above $60,000. Ethereum also rebounded, swinging between $1,600 and $1,720. Altcoins outpaced Bitcoin on the bounce: the CoinDesk DeFi Select Index gained 6.3% and the Infrastructure Select Index 6.6%, roughly double Bitcoin's 3.2% advance. Yet derivatives trading volume jumped 16.7% over 24 hours to $236.2 billion, suggesting leverage did much of the lifting. [Source: CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/26/bitcoin-little-changed-defi-tokens-lead-the-bounce-as-u-s-marks-thanksgiving)

## ETF Outflows: November Net Outflows of $5.39 Billion, a Monthly Record

While prices rebounded, institutional money moved in the opposite direction. U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs saw $453.1 million in net outflows on November 25, a seventh straight session of outflows. November net outflows now total $5.39 billion — the largest monthly withdrawal since the spot ETFs launched. BlackRock's IBIT accounted for $330 million of the day's outflows, most of the total. Spot Bitcoin ETF net assets have fallen to $72.03 billion from $104.27 billion on October 29. [Source: CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/26/bitcoin-little-changed-defi-tokens-lead-the-bounce-as-u-s-marks-thanksgiving)

Spot Ether ETFs also recorded $68.5 million in net outflows on November 25, an eighth straight session of outflows. November net outflows total $1.29 billion, and the entire day's outflow came from BlackRock's ETHA. [Source: LinkedIn/Exness FM](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/bitcoin-prices-bounce-off-pce-shock-long-term-holders-tahir-8vqmc)

On-chain data also shows cooling sentiment. Bitcoin's SOPR (spent output profit ratio) ended at 0.980, below 1 for a third consecutive week, meaning investors moving coins are on average realizing losses. Seven-day realized losses rose to $10.63 billion, a weekly pace not seen since February. The share of Bitcoin supply in profit fell to 46.5%, while Ether's slipped to 29.9% — roughly 7 in 10 ETH sit underwater. [Source: LinkedIn/Exness FM](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/bitcoin-prices-bounce-off-pce-shock-long-term-holders-tahir-8vqmc)

## Questions and Answers

### Bitcoin recovered above $60,000 — why is money still leaving the ETFs?

The rebound was driven mainly by short covering and leveraged funds in the derivatives market, while institutional money via ETFs recorded a seventh straight session of net outflows. With price and fund flows pointing in opposite directions, whether ETF flows turn positive is a key signal for the durability of the rebound.

### How might the November 27 options expiry affect the market?

Roughly $7 billion in Bitcoin options expire, with the most-traded contract being a $57,000 put expiring November 27. With U.S. markets on a shortened Thanksgiving schedule and liquidity thin, price swings could be larger than usual around expiry.

### What does a Fear & Greed Index reading of 10 mean?

On a 0–100 scale of investor sentiment, 10 is in "extreme fear" territory. It signals excessive pessimism, but on its own it does not guarantee a bottom. Together with ETF outflows and on-chain loss data, it shows sentiment has not yet recovered.

### What would the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve bill mean if it passes?

The bill would lock up U.S. government-held Bitcoin for 20 years as a reserve asset. It has so far only cleared the House Financial Services Committee; it still needs a full House vote and Senate approval, so it is not yet settled policy. If enacted, it would structurally reduce selling pressure from government Bitcoin holdings.
