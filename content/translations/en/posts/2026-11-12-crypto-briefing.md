## SEC Approves Grayscale Index ETF Conversion

On November 11, the SEC approved the proposal to convert the Grayscale Digital Large Cap Fund (GDLC) into an ETF. The fund tracks a basket of the five largest cryptocurrencies by market capitalization, with weights at approval of roughly 80% Bitcoin, 11% Ethereum, 4.8% XRP, 2.8% Solana, and 0.8% Cardano. Assets under management are about $775 million, and it will list on NYSE Arca. [OKX News](https://tr.okx.com/en/orbit/news/sec-approves-grayscale-index-etf-conversion-clears-solana-xrp-cardano-for-spot-trading-48462547697696) reported that the approval clears the way for spot ETF trading in Solana, XRP, and Cardano.

Bitwise has also filed to convert its $1.3 billion Bitwise 10 Crypto Index Fund (BITW) into an exchange-traded product. Following the Bitcoin and Ethereum spot ETFs, major altcoins are now entering the regulated ETF framework. But prices barely moved on the day of the approval. The market is reacting more sensitively to the immediate headwinds — interest rates and ETF flows — than to the structural tailwind.

## December Rate-Hike Fears, the Market's Biggest Burden

The Fed's rate path has become the market's biggest burden. October CPI rose 0.4% month over month, beating expectations of 0.3%, and October nonfarm payrolls added 165,000 jobs, far above the roughly 65,000 expected. Rate-hike expectations have jumped sharply as a result. According to [CryptoNews](https://cryptonews.com/news/bitcoin-price-gensler-2-fears-fed-crypto-bank/), the CME FedWatch tool now puts the odds of a 25-basis-point hike in December at 86.3%.

Rate-hike fears weigh on risk assets broadly. After the Nasdaq fell 5.5% on November 5, Bitcoin also lost its 50-week moving average. [CryptoNews](https://cryptonews.com/news/bitcoin-price-gensler-2-fears-fed-crypto-bank/) reported that about $732 million in crypto positions were forcibly liquidated over 24 hours on November 11, with roughly 79% of them long positions. Liquidations begetting liquidations is amplifying short-term volatility.

## ETF Outflows Extend to a Fourth Day

Institutional outflows have not stopped either. [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/11/bitcoin-sits-out-stock-rebound-as-etf-outflows-extend-to-fourth-day) reported on November 11 that US spot Bitcoin ETF outflows extended to a fourth straight trading day. About $1.28 billion left spot Bitcoin ETFs in the week of November 2–6, and Ethereum ETFs lost about $365 million over the same period. BlackRock's IBIT had recorded seven straight days of outflows as of November 9.

Regulatory uncertainty after the midterms also lingers in the background. With Democrats now controlling both chambers of Congress, the outlook for the crypto market-structure bill (CLARITY) has darkened, and some in the industry are even raising the possibility of a "Gensler 2.0"-style return to tougher regulation. That said, no new rules have been enacted, and the market's concerns remain in the realm of expectations and forecasts — a distinction worth keeping. The Fear & Greed Index stands at 29 (Fear), showing sentiment is still depressed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does the Grayscale index ETF approval matter?

After the Bitcoin and Ethereum spot ETFs, XRP, Solana, and Cardano are now included in a regulated ETF product. Investors can gain exposure to a basket rather than a single coin, and other index products — such as Bitwise's — are following with their own conversion filings. Still, prices did not react on the day, and the scale of actual inflows will be the key question.

### With December hike odds this high, could Bitcoin fall further?

Short-term downside pressure could persist while hike expectations stay elevated. If Bitcoin fails to reclaim its 50-week moving average (around $76,100), some analysts warn of a move toward $70,000. Conversely, if the Fed's December 9 decision softens hike expectations, or ETF flows turn positive, that could set up a rebound. Neither outcome is a confirmed fact.

### What would it take for ETF outflows to stop?

Institutional risk aversion needs to ease. That would require fading rate-hike expectations, signs of cooling inflation, or inflows into new products such as the Grayscale index ETF. As long as outflows continue, spot selling pressure will persist.
