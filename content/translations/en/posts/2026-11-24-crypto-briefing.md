## From the Weekend Low to $61K… The Rebound Still Looks Like a Bounce

Bitcoin fell to $55,130 on Saturday — its lowest since September 2024 — then recovered as high as $61,568 over the weekend. It traded above $60,000 on Monday, up more than 10% from the low, but is still down 5% on the week. [FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/cryptocurrencies/news/top-3-price-prediction-bitcoin-ethereum-ripple-btc-eth-and-xrp-remain-vulnerable-as-bears-drive-prices-toward-yearly-lows-202611230352) reported that Bitcoin remains well below its 200-day moving average with a daily RSI of 26.6, in oversold territory, and that the technical trend has not yet repaired itself.

Altcoins bounced more sharply. Solana, down 31% for November, rose 3.7% in a day to reclaim the $72 range; XRP recovered from last week's yearly low of $0.86 to $0.94, and Dogecoin and Cardano both gained more than 2%. Even so, November's cumulative losses — Bitcoin down about 25%, Ethereum about 20% and Solana 31% — mean a day or two of gains has only clawed back part of the month's damage. According to [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/news/benzinga:2cbd5b2c8094b:0-bitcoin-ethereum-rise-but-xrp-dogecoin-dip-amid-crypto-s-punishing-november-analyst-flags-btc-s-vital-move-to-the-upside/), more than $250 million in crypto positions were liquidated over the past 24 hours, with short liquidations helping to fuel the rebound.

## ETF Outflows Top $1 Billion for a Third Straight Week… Institutional Money Has Not Returned

Unlike prices, institutional flows remain negative. According to [Investorideas](https://www.investorideas.com/news/2026/cryptocurrency/11231-bitcoins-oversold-rebound-faces-test-as-spot-etf-outflows-exceed-1-2bn-for-third-consecutive-week.asp), U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded about $1.24 billion in net outflows during November 16–20, marking a third consecutive week of outflows above $1 billion. Spot Ethereum ETFs lost $222 million over the same period. Bitcoin ETF outflows for November 2–13 alone reached $2.22 billion, with BlackRock's IBIT accounting for about 83% of that total.

There are also signs of heavy selling pressure from U.S. investors. The Coinbase Bitcoin Premium Index fell as low as −0.23%, meaning Bitcoin traded at a discount in the U.S. market versus other exchanges. Until institutional buying returns, the rebound's staying power is far from assured.

## Why Rate Hike Fears Persist… Wednesday's PCE Is the Watershed

The macro backdrop remains a headwind. After hawkish remarks from Fed Governors Christopher Waller and Lisa Cook, markets are pricing an 88–95% probability of a 25-basis-point hike at the December 8–9 FOMC meeting. October core CPI rose 0.3% month over month, meeting the condition Waller set for considering a further hike, which has reinforced expectations of a December move. The Fed's current policy rate is 3.75–4.00%.

This week, attention is on October PCE inflation, due Wednesday (November 25). Because it is the Fed's preferred inflation gauge, a reading above forecasts could strengthen expectations of a December hike and prolong weakness in risk assets. The second estimate of third-quarter GDP is also due the same day; Thursday is Thanksgiving, when U.S. markets are closed, and Friday is a shortened session. New York Fed President John Williams is scheduled to speak today.

Other items to watch include the Solana Foundation's launch of 'solana.ai', an AI agent development platform; Upbit and Bithumb suspending CYBER deposits and withdrawals after unexpected additional token minting on the Solana chain; and Chainflip offering the hacker behind a $12 million theft a 10% bounty in exchange for returning the funds. Ethereum developers have begun reconsidering the Glamsterdam upgrade timeline over compatibility issues tied to proposed gas-pricing changes. A MON token unlock equal to 32.37% of circulating supply (about $219 million) is also scheduled for today, so volatility in related tokens bears watching.
