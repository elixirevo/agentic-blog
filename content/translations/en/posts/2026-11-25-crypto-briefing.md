## The Nvidia-Driven Rebound Vanished Within a Day

This week's market action can be summed up as a Monday rally followed by a Tuesday reversal. Bitcoin fell to $55,130 over the weekend — its lowest level in 26 months — then surged as high as $64,446 on Monday as tech stocks jumped on news of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's return. But the rally did not last a day. According to [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/) and the [CoinDesk live blog](https://www.monochromechronicles.com/section-crypto), Nasdaq 100 futures fell about 2%, pulling Bitcoin back toward $60,000, while Ethereum, XRP, and Solana fell even harder than Bitcoin in morning trading. Bitcoin traded between a 24-hour high of $61,592 and a low of $59,197 and is now changing hands below $60,000.

## Spot ETF Outflows Near $5 Billion in November

Fund flows confirm the downturn. U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs saw about $570 million in net outflows on Monday, November 23 alone, with BlackRock's IBIT accounting for $509.3 million of that total. According to [Phemex's market briefing](https://phemex.com/news/article/phemex-crypto-market-daily-bitcoin-slides-below-604k-as-etf-outflows-mount-ahead-of-pce-nov-24-104700), that marked a fifth straight session of net outflows, with November's cumulative outflows approaching $5 billion. That extends a four-week streak of withdrawals that had already removed more than $4 billion, and the exodus has not stopped this week.

Still, there are buyers on the other side. Strategy bought another 457 BTC — about $29.6 million — between November 16 and 22 at an average price of $64,898, lifting its holdings to 851,305 BTC. Large derivatives traders have also been spotted betting on a rebound near the $60,000 level. Grayscale described Bitcoin as oversold after its drop to $55,000 on November 21.

## Rate-Hike Fears Are Weighing on the Market

The macro backdrop remains unfavorable for crypto. Growing conviction that the Federal Reserve will raise rates in December is pressuring risk assets across the board. Some investors also withdrew from Bitcoin ETFs ahead of Anthropic's initial public offering last week. With Democrats set to take control of Congress starting in 2027, concerns about tougher U.S. crypto regulation are also surfacing. [An analysis by TalkRed](https://www.talkred.com/2026/11/bitcoin-sinks-below-60000-as-rate-and-regulatory-fears-tighten-their-grip/) notes that rate-hike expectations, regulatory concerns, and ETF outflows together raise the odds of a second straight monthly decline for crypto.

## Regulation and Institutional News: CFTC Guidance and Progress in Korea and Europe

Even in a falling market, institutional adoption news continued. The U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) issued guidance on November 23 allowing Bitcoin, Ethereum, and payment stablecoins to count toward derivatives firms' 12-month operating reserves. A 20% haircut applies to Bitcoin and Ethereum, and 2% to stablecoins.

In South Korea, a draft Digital Asset Basic Act was unveiled, and the European Central Bank (ECB) approved accepting on-chain issued assets as collateral starting in June 2027. Payoneer also received preliminary conditional approval from the U.S. Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC) to form a national trust bank that would issue the GENIUS Act-compliant stablecoin PAYO-USD. OKX's tokenized securities platform OKXICE began operating on November 23 with 113 assets under U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) approval.
