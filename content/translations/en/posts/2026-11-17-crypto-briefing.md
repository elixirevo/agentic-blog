## About $2 Billion in Weekend Liquidations Accelerated the Drop

Leveraged liquidations drove the speed of the decline. According to CoinGlass, roughly $2 billion in crypto positions were forcibly liquidated over a 24-hour period over the weekend, mostly longs. [Cointelegraph coverage](https://www.fxgaininfo.com/market-news/crypto/bitcoin-falls-to-four-month-low-near-62k-as-crypto-selloff-deepens/) reports that about 91% of the liquidations were long positions, with Bitcoin liquidations alone reaching around $750 million. Once the flush ended, dip buyers stepped in and recouped part of the losses. Nansen research analyst Nicolai Søndergaard noted that Bitcoin perpetual futures open interest on Hyperliquid has fallen about 17% since October 23, with funding rates down to around 3.5% annualized, as leverage leaves the market.
