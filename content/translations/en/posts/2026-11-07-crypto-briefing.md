## Today's Key Stories

### 1. Jobs Surprise: October Nonfarm Payrolls Up 165,000, 2.5x Expectations

The US Bureau of Labor Statistics reported on November 6 that nonfarm payrolls rose by 165,000 in October, far above market expectations of about 65,000. The unemployment rate fell from 4.2% to 4.1%, and August and September payrolls were revised up by a combined 84,000. ([Crypto Times](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/11/06/bitcoin-and-ethereum-slip-as-us-jobs-report-shows-165k-new-jobs/), [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/06/bitcoin-steadies-near-usd76-000-after-ai-led-selloff))

Strong hiring led markets to raise the odds of a Fed rate hike again. Market-implied probability of a 25bp December hike rose from about 67% to 77%. Bitcoin fell to $74,722 on Coinbase right after the report — its lowest since August 21 — before rebounding toward $75,900. ([Bitcoin Insider](https://www.bitcoininsider.org/article/bitcoin-lowest-since-august-hot-october-jobs-report))

The jobs report alone does not establish the cause of the decline. It is a snapshot of employment conditions and does not itself determine the Fed's next decision. The next jobs report is due December 4, and the FOMC meets December 8–9.

## Connecting the News to the Price

This week's action reads in three stages. First, **the post-midterm rebound was reversed by a regulatory repricing**. Prices reclaimed $80,000 on November 4, then turned lower within a day as news of the House Democrats' bill discussions spread. Second, **the tech rout triggered a liquidation cascade**. The November 5 Nasdaq plunge came with more than $1 billion in long liquidations, deepening the drop. Third, **the jobs surprise flipped rate expectations**. The November 6 employment report strengthened rate-hike bets, pushing Bitcoin to $74,722 before it steadied near $76,000.

Nothing structural has changed yet. ETF outflows have run for two weeks and institutional demand is cooling, but BlackRock's long-term crypto strategy is unchanged and the CFTC's derivatives deregulation is proceeding. In the near term, the $75,000 support level and Friday's ETF flows will set the direction.

## Expected Questions and Answers

### Why is Bitcoin holding around $75,000?

The area near $75,000 is close to the November 3 low and the 50-week moving average, so it is acting as technical support. After dipping to $74,722 right after the jobs report on November 6, buying came in and pushed the price back toward $76,000. Holding support does not guarantee the decline is over.

### Why did Bitcoin fall when the jobs data was strong?

Strong hiring raises the chance the Fed hikes rates or keeps them high, in the market's reading. Higher rates make risk assets relatively less attractive and strengthen the dollar. After the November 6 release, the implied probability of a December hike rose from 67% to 77%, and Bitcoin fell to its lowest since August during the session.

### ETF money keeps leaving — what would signal a reversal?

Bitcoin and Ethereum spot ETFs lost $530 million in a single day on November 5, and the weekly outflow streak has reached two weeks. A reversal can only be judged after watching flows for several more days. There was a small net inflow on November 4, but outflows resumed the next day, so a single day of inflows is not enough to confirm a trend change. This article is not investment advice.
