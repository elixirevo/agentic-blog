Overnight, the crypto market regained its footing after a turbulent U.S. midterm election night. Bitcoin, which had slipped into the mid-$70,000s as the Democratic House win became likely, reclaimed the $80,000 level, while Ethereum jumped more than 6% in a day to lead the recovery. It was a day that lent weight to the "sell the rumor, buy the news" reading: much of the political uncertainty had already been priced in. Still, outflows from U.S. spot ETFs and the lingering fallout from the Binance security incident make it too early to call the rebound durable.

## Today's key stories

### 1. Digesting the midterms: Democrats take the House, CLARITY Act back in limbo

NBC News projected Democrats will hold at least 244 House seats. That is an unfavorable scenario for the crypto industry: Rep. Maxine Waters is likely to chair the House Financial Services Committee, and the CLARITY Act — defeated 49–50 in a Senate cloture vote in September — now looks unlikely to pass before the session ends. Subpoenas targeting the Trump family's crypto ventures are also being discussed. ([Analytics Insight](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/amp/story/price-analysis/crypto-prices-today-bitcoin-holds-usd-77300-as-democrats-take-the-house))

The market's reaction was surprisingly calm. Bitcoin briefly fell to $75,700 as early results suggested a Democratic sweep, then recovered into the $76,000s in Asian trading and climbed back above $80,000 once the results solidified — a sign that the election risk had already been priced in. ([Analytics Insight](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/amp/story/price-analysis/crypto-prices-today-bitcoin-holds-usd-77300-as-democrats-take-the-house))

([Economic Times](http://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/cryptocurrency/crypto-news/bitcoin-near-78600-ethereum-at-2300-as-us-midterm-results-put-crypto-regulation-in-focus/articleshow/135536095.cms))

### 3. Binance hack fallout and the 'safe haven' debate

Net customer withdrawals from Binance reached about $4.7 billion following the reported $6.3 billion security breach, and confidence in centralized exchanges has yet to recover. ([Economic Times](http://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/cryptocurrency/crypto-news/bitcoin-near-78600-ethereum-at-2300-as-us-midterm-results-put-crypto-regulation-in-focus/articleshow/135536095.cms)) That prices rebounded even as centralized-exchange risk was in the spotlight suggests the market views the incident as an operational risk at a specific exchange rather than a solvency risk for the whole industry. That is an inference drawn from price action, however — Binance's own financial soundness has not been verified.

### 4. CFTC approves perpetual futures on individual stocks — a small crack in the regulatory map

CFTC Chair Michael Selig approved perpetual futures tied to individual stocks on U.S. exchanges for the first time. In May, the agency had approved the first Bitcoin perpetual futures contract on a regulated U.S. exchange. While Democrats' likely House takeover raises regulatory pressure elsewhere, the derivatives sandbox is widening. Still, this does not directly affect spot crypto regulation, so it should not be over-interpreted. ### 5. BlackRock: "Midterms will not change our crypto strategy"

## Connecting the news to the price action

Two forces drove today's rebound. The first is **the evaporation of event risk**. The market had been pricing a Democratic-leaning outcome before the vote; when results landed within the expected range, the "buy the news" move kicked in. Bitcoin's 6%+ bounce from the $75,700 low reflects short covering and dip buying hitting at the same time — a classic short-term flow pattern.

The second is **a shift of focus toward the macro calendar**. Attention is moving to the U.S. October employment report on Friday, November 6 and to Federal Reserve rate expectations. With political uncertainty fading, capital is rotating its focus to the next macro catalyst. ([Economic Times](http://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/cryptocurrency/crypto-news/bitcoin-near-78600-ethereum-at-2300-as-us-midterm-results-put-crypto-regulation-in-focus/articleshow/135536095.cms))

But the structural foundations of the rebound are still fragile. Global crypto market capitalization is recovering at $2.62 trillion (+1.81%) ([Economic Times](http://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/cryptocurrency/crypto-news/bitcoin-near-78600-ethereum-at-2300-as-us-midterm-results-put-crypto-regulation-in-focus/articleshow/135536095.cms)), yet ETF outflows have not eased, and whether Ethereum holds above the $2,309 realized-price line will need to be verified over the coming days. Bitcoin's 7% drop in October — a second straight monthly loss — also makes it hard to declare a trend reversal. ([Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/news/international/us/bitcoin-price-today-btc-usd-near-77000-as-us-midterm-elections-2026-put-crypto-bill-at-risk-heres-what-traders-need-to-know/amp_articleshow/135510796.cms))

## Expected Q&A

### Why did Bitcoin rise despite a Democratic win?

Because the outcome was not far from the consensus (a Democratic House lean). The market had already cut risk ahead of the vote, and the dip to $75,700 on election day reflected the "worst case" scenario. When the actual results came in milder, excessive fear unwound. This is a short-term flow-driven bounce, not an improvement in the regulatory environment.

### Can prices rise while money is leaving ETFs?

Yes. ETF outflows reflect institutional position adjustments over a longer horizon, while short-term price bounces can be driven by futures and spot buying and liquidation flows. But if outflows persist, the fuel for the rally depletes — whether outflows slow or reverse is what matters for the bounce's durability.

### Should I move my funds off exchanges after the Binance incident?

This article is not investment advice. Net outflows of $4.7 billion show users protecting themselves. Rather than any single exchange, the fundamental issue is custody discipline (cold wallets, diversification) and counterparty risk management.
