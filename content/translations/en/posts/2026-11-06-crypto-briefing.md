The joy of reclaiming $80,000 lasted less than a day. Bitcoin rose as high as $80,918 during trading on November 5 (US time) before sliding sharply back to the $75,000 range, while Ethereum fell more than 8% and dragged the broader altcoin market down with it. Nearly $1 billion in leveraged positions was liquidated within a day, and most top-cap coins dropped 4–8%. Regulatory uncertainty after the midterms is spreading into fresh legislative debate rather than resolving, the detailed report on the Binance hack shook market confidence again, and caution ahead of tonight's US jobs report added to the pressure.

## Today's Key Stories

### 1. A day of reversal: back to the $75,000s after retaking $80,000

Bitcoin changed direction a day after leading a rebound that reclaimed $80,000 on November 4. The price had climbed from $77,345 on November 3 to $80,405 on November 4, then hit an intraday high of $80,881 on November 5 before sliding to $75,572. ([Aktualita](https://aktualita.co/en/economy/btc-usd-today-november-6-2026-bitcoin-plummets-to-us76000-what-is-the-cause)) Ethereum also gave back the gains that had taken it to $2,425, and XRP and Dogecoin swung from roughly 4% 24-hour gains to drops of more than 7%. ([Crypto News Focus](https://cryptonewsfocus.com/bitcoin-breaks-80k-as-ethereum-xrp-and-dogecoin-join-the-crypto-recovery/))

In derivatives markets, 129,472 traders were liquidated over 24 hours, wiping out about $983 million in positions. ([Crypto News Focus](https://cryptonewsfocus.com/bitcoin-breaks-80k-as-ethereum-xrp-and-dogecoin-join-the-crypto-recovery/)) It is the classic pattern of long leverage built up during a rebound being flushed out in a cascade as prices fall. The low-$75,000 area is near the November 3 low, so the market is now testing short-term support.

### 2. ETF flows turn to inflows for the first time in November — yet prices fall

US spot ETF flows showed a small sign of turning. According to SoSoValue data, on November 4 spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded $74.57 million in net inflows and spot Ethereum ETFs $53.02 million — the first positive day of November for both. Before that, Bitcoin ETFs had lost $643 million on October 30, $399 million on November 2 and $115.6 million on November 3. ([CoinGabbar](https://www.coingabbar.com/en/crypto-etf-news-today-bitcoin-ethereum-inflow-xrp-sol-update), [Crypto News Focus](https://cryptonewsfocus.com/bitcoin-breaks-80k-as-ethereum-xrp-and-dogecoin-join-the-crypto-recovery/))

Still, the inflows are small next to the outflows that preceded them, and prices fell even on the inflow day, so it is too early to call a reversal in flows. XRP and Solana ETFs recorded no flows that day. ([CoinGabbar](https://www.coingabbar.com/en/crypto-etf-news-today-bitcoin-ethereum-inflow-xrp-sol-update))

### 3. Binance hack detailed report: a six-month infiltration, $6.3 billion stolen

In its initial investigation report published November 3, Binance said the $6.34 billion hack of October 29 was the culmination of an infiltration campaign that began in June. Attackers compromised the computers of three engineers in turn — through a tampered Homebrew installer, malware hidden in a fake job interview, and an internal AI tool — then used stolen deployment credentials to reach the wallet-signing service and bypass the normal withdrawal process. On Ethereum, they exploited EIP-7702 signatures to seize control of a hot wallet. ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/cybersecurity/2026/binance-reports-hackers-infiltrated-systems-months-before-stealing-6-billion-dollars/))

The stolen assets included 18,270 BTC, 233.46 million XRP and about 970,500 SOL, and the FBI has attributed the attack to North Korean hackers (North Korea denies it). Binance said about $2.85 billion worth has been frozen or intercepted and 98% of affected tokens have been replenished. ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/cybersecurity/2026/binance-reports-hackers-infiltrated-systems-months-before-stealing-6-billion-dollars/)) The exchange says customers will be fully compensated, but a week after the incident, market wariness over centralized-exchange risk has not faded.

## Connecting the News to the Price Action

Today's drop is not the work of a single event but of three pressures overlapping. First, **a post-election regulatory repricing**: the market bet on "event risk gone" to retake $80,000, but news that House Democrats are discussing a bill to expand SEC oversight put a regulatory premium back into prices. Second, **the aftershock of the Binance hack report**: the incident itself is a week old, but the detailed disclosure of the infiltration path and scale is slowing the recovery of trust in exchanges. Third, **risk aversion ahead of the jobs report** and **a cascade of leveraged liquidations**: long positions built up during the rebound were flushed out as prices fell, amplifying the decline.

Nothing has changed structurally yet, however. ETF flows turned net positive for the first time in November, BlackRock's long-term strategy is unchanged, and the CFTC's derivatives deregulation is moving forward. In the short term, whether the low-$75,000 area holds and what tonight's jobs report shows will set the direction.

## Questions and Answers

### Why did Bitcoin fall a day after reclaiming $80,000?

No single cause explains it; several factors overlapped. A repricing of regulation toward potentially tighter oversight after the election, the detailed Binance hack report, caution ahead of the jobs report, and a cascade of liquidations of leveraged long positions built up during the rebound all played a part. If yesterday's rally was short-term flows riding on "event risk gone," today's drop is that flow reversing.

### Can prices fall even when money flows into ETFs?

Yes. The November 4 inflows (about $75 million) were small compared with the more than $1.1 billion that flowed out over the previous three sessions, and short-term supply and demand in spot and futures markets can outweigh ETF flows. Whether the inflows mark a reversal will take several more days to judge.

### How much does the Binance hack matter for the whole market?

The scale is among the largest ever, but Binance says it will fully compensate customers and has replenished 98% of affected tokens, so the incident has not spread into industry-wide solvency risk. It has, however, put centralized-exchange security and operational risk in focus and raised interest in self-custody (cold wallets, diversified storage). This is not investment advice.
