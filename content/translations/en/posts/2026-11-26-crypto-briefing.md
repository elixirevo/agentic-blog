## Frequently Asked Questions

### Bitcoin recovered $60,000 — has the bottom been confirmed?

Not yet. Bitcoin bounced from $57,300, but ETF outflows continue and rate-hike fears have not faded. The key tests are whether it can hold above $60,500 and whether the $57,000 support is tested again.

### Why does PCE inflation affect crypto?

PCE is the Fed's preferred inflation gauge. Hot inflation weakens expectations for rate cuts and raises the chance of a hike, which is negative for risk assets like crypto. Bitcoin's drop below $58,000 right after the October release illustrates this.

### Will prices fall further if ETF outflows continue?

Outflows are themselves direct selling pressure, so they weigh on prices in the short term. However, the pace slowed from $570 million on Monday to $231 million on Tuesday. A turn to net inflows would be an early signal of a rebound.

### Why did altcoins fall more than Bitcoin?

In risk-off phases, less liquid and more volatile altcoins are sold first. On November 25, Solana and Hyperliquid fell more than Bitcoin, and Ethereum is heading for its worst month since February 2025.
