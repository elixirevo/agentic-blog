## Connecting the News to Price Action

![Illustration of a blockchain network with a rising trend line, symbolizing market analysis](/uploads/blog/crypto-network-2026-11-04.webp)

Two forces are driving today's market. The first is **a repricing of political risk**. Markets appeared to be betting on a Republican-leaning scenario ahead of the midterms, but as Democratic victory projections solidified, expectations of a friendly regulatory environment faded and Bitcoin dropped 2.6%. The likely demise of the CLARITY Act directly raises business uncertainty in the US.

The second is **persistent liquidity withdrawal**. Since the Binance hack, ETF outflows have continued at a record monthly pace ($3.79 billion from spot Bitcoin ETFs in November per Morningstar), and leveraged longs were flushed out in derivatives markets. Yet Bitcoin's rebound from the $75,000s and BTC dominance holding at 59.3% suggest capital is rotating into Bitcoin over altcoins — a relative safe-haven preference within risk-off.

The quality of the rebound remains weak, though. Bitcoin sits below its 100-day moving average ($77,776), and Ethereum cannot clear the $2,309 realized price. Some analysts see a "sell the rumor, buy the news" setup, judging that technical upside could open once election uncertainty lifts.

## Anticipated Questions

### Why did Bitcoin react so sensitively to the election result?

The US is the world's largest crypto market and the regulatory reference point. Which party holds power determines whether stablecoin and market-structure bills pass, directly affecting the business environment for exchanges, issuers, and institutional investors. Projected Democratic wins lowered expectations of friendly regulation, triggering short-term selling.

### Will prices fall further if ETF outflows continue?

Spot ETFs are the conduit for institutional demand, so persistent net outflows are selling pressure. But slowing outflows — or a flip to net inflows — can mark a bottom. With November outflows at record levels, watching for a reversal in the flow matters.

### Is this a buying opportunity?

This article is not investment advice. Technically, Bitcoin is testing its 200-day moving average as support, and some see a rebound scenario once political uncertainty clears. Conversely, worsening regulation and continued outflows are further downside risks. Decide within your own risk tolerance.
